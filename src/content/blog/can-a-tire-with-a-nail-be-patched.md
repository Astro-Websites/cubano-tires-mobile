## Where the nail is matters most

The tread, the part that touches the road, is thick and can hold a repair. The shoulder and sidewall flex every time the tire rolls, so a repair there won't hold. That's why the same nail can be a quick fix in one spot and a new tire in another.

* **Usually repairable:** nail or screw in the tread, about ¼" or smaller.
* **Needs a new tire:** sidewall or shoulder damage, cuts bigger than ¼", or a tire driven on flat.

## What to do right now

1. Leave the nail in. It's often plugging the hole.
2. Check the pressure. If it's holding, drive gently and only as far as you need to.
3. If it's flat, don't drive on it. Driving flat can ruin a tire that was repairable.
4. Snap a photo of where the nail is and text it to us.

## How a proper repair is done

At Cubano Tires Mobile Shop, we use professional plug-patch repairs to safely repair punctured tires. We start by removing the tire from the wheel and inspecting the inside to make sure the damage is repairable. Once approved, we install a plug-patch combination from the inside of the tire to properly seal the puncture. We then remount the tire, balance the wheel, and inflate it to the manufacturer's recommended tire pressure before completing the service. Tire repairs start at $35 and up, depending on tire size, plus the mobile service fee.
