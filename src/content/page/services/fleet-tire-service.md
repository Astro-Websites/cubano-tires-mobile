    question: Do you come to our yard?

  - _template: faq
    question: Can you bill our company instead of the driver paying?
    answer: Yes. That's what a fleet account is for. Work gets billed to your company so drivers don't pay at the truck. We offer Net 30 and Net 45 invoicing terms, with purchase orders (POs).
  - _template: faq
    question: Do you do DOT tire inspections?
    answer: Yes. We provide DOT-style tire inspections for commercial fleets, checking tread, pressure and damage across your vehicles and telling you what's due. As a rule of thumb, federal rules call for at least 4/32" of tread on a commercial truck's steer tires and 2/32" on the other positions. Check FMCSA rules for your vehicles.
  - _template: faq
    question: How soon can you get to us?
